1. Introduction

Welcome to Vital Health ("we," "our," or "us"). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. This Privacy Policy explains how our mobile application handles your information when you use Vital Health.

2. Information We Collect and Process

Vital Health is designed with your privacy in mind:

  • Camera Data (Facial Scan): When you use the "Check Health Status" feature, the app accesses your device's front camera in real-time to analyze facial expressions (such as smile probability and eye openness) using on-device machine learning (Google ML Kit). We do not record, store, capture, or transmit any photos, videos, images, or biometric data to any remote servers. All processing happens locally on your device in real-time and is immediately discarded.
  • Personal Notes and Health Records: Any custom notes you write or automatic health scan results saved within the "Notes and Health Records" screen are stored locally on your device using local storage preferences. We do not access, upload, or sync your notes to external databases.

3. Permissions Requested

  • Camera (android.permission.CAMERA): Required solely for the real-time wellness and aura status verification feature. You can revoke this permission at any time in your device settings.
  • Internet (android.permission.INTERNET): Used solely for standard network connectivity (if applicable to updates or basic library requirements).

4. Third-Party Services

We do not share any data with third parties, as we do not collect personal data. The app utilizes Google ML Kit for on-device facial analysis, which operates entirely offline on your device.

5. Children's Privacy

Our app does not knowingly collect any personal information from children under the age of 13. Since no personal data is collected or transmitted, the app is safe for users of all ages.
